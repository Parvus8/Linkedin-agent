# Style rules
- The first line must be a strong hook; it's all people see before "see more".
- Short paragraphs of 1-2 lines, with blank lines between them.
- Between 600 and 1300 characters.
- At most 3 hashtags, at the end, in CamelCase without underscores (e.g. #EngenhariaDeDados).
- At most 2 emojis, or none.
- No links in the body of the post.

# Never
- Never invent personal stories, clients, projects, or experiences.
- Never invent statistics, numbers, or quotes.
- No clickbait, no "engagement bait" ("comment YES if you agree").
- No buzzword-heavy text ("disruptive", "game changer", "synergy").
